\caption{Proposed installation and verification sequence. Contact-aware
control and passive fixtures support each physical operation. A failed
continuity test triggers diagnosis and recovery, by either a retry or a
relocation. The survey returns the feed pose and radiator shape with their
uncertainties. Ground trials will measure task completion, recovery time and
final electrical validity.}
